Realms on the Rails

Realms on the Rails is a Multiplayer Roguelike video game that was developed among a group of seven students at Drury University. You and up to three other players band together to plunder a train with boxcars that serve as portals to other dimensions. Each car has its own objective and treasures galore. Using their gold and magic playing cards, players work their way to The Conductor to take over the train.

The Cards

These are the illustrations for the magic card abilities players may use in the game. When designing the cards, it was important for the illustration to show the player what it does. As well as stay true to the art style of the game and maintain consistency. All digitally hand drawn, painted, and designed by me.

Particles

I created the particles for multiple assets. I never touched Unreal Engine's particle system, but it was a lot of fun to learn the little I did. These are some previews of the particles implemented into the game.
